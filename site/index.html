timecrunchy

Training zones from your ramp test FTP or threshold heart rate, and week-by-week plans for racing, Gran Fondos, commuting and more. Every ride for the trainer is also a .zwo file you can load into a trainer app.

Workouts for apps

The .zwo files set ERG targets in the middle of each zone (EM 65%, SS 98%, CR 108% of FTP) and 120% FTP for PowerIntervals; Peak-and-Fade PowerIntervals use a 6% slope instead, because they start all out and fade. Group rides, commutes, races and cyclocross practice are outdoors and have no file.

Everything is also available as JSON: v1/plans.json lists the plans, v1/plans/<id>.json has every ride of a plan, v1/zones.json the zones. The timecrunchy.js module has the same functions.

Zones and plans from The Time-Crunched Cyclist by Chris Carmichael (3rd edition), adapted to a ramp test FTP and threshold heart rate by pilippopilippo. More in the post Time-crunched training.
